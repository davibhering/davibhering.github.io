Davi Bhering

Ph.D. Candidate in Economics · Paris School of Economics

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the Paris School of Economics and Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University. My research examines how tax and transfer systems shape economic behavior and inequality when institutional and administrative capacity is limited. Building partnerships with public institutions, I leverage novel administrative data to estimate behavioral responses and combine these estimates with economic models to quantify their broader implications.

In Fall 2024, I visited UC Berkeley with Prof. Emmanuel Saez.
In Fall 2027, I will visit Columbia University with Prof. Michael Best.
